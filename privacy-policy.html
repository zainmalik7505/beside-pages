Privacy Policy

Beside Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Beside respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information Beside collects, how it is used, and what choices you have.

1. Information we do not collect

Beside does not require an account, does not collect personal user profiles, and does not sell or share personal data for advertising or marketing purposes. We do not use third-party advertising analytics in the app.

We do not upload your files, documents, photos, notes, or extracted text to a remote service for general analytics.

2. Information stored on your device

Beside stores your imported files, notes, thumbnails, and related local data on your device for app functionality. This data is used to support features such as:

  • Saving and organizing files, photos, PDFs, and links
  • Extracting text from supported documents and images
  • Searching your saved content locally
  • Keeping notes and comparisons alongside your saved items

3. How we handle extracted text

If you use text extraction features, the extracted text is processed on-device to support editing, searching, and note creation. This text remains local to your device and is not used to create a user profile or account.

4. App permissions

Beside may request access to device features that are necessary for its core functionality, such as:

  • Camera or photo library access for importing images or scans
  • Files access for opening and organizing local files
  • Local data access required for storing saved items on-device

These permissions are used only to provide the features you choose to use in the app.

5. Links and web browsing

Beside may open external web links that you save or choose to view. External websites are governed by those websites' own privacy policies and practices. Beside does not control or monitor activity on third-party websites.

6. Data retention

Information stored in Beside remains on your device until you delete it or uninstall the app. If you remove items, they are deleted from the app's local storage.

7. No third-party analytics or advertising

Beside does not use third-party advertising identifiers, ad networks, or user analytics services for tracking or profiling.

8. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how Beside handles your data, please contact us at zainworkmaill@gmail.com.

This policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes in the app or applicable law. Continued use of the app after changes are made means you accept the updated policy.
