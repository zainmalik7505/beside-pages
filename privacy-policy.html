Privacy Policy

Privacy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Beside is designed to be a local, private way to keep your documents, notes, and saved links organized. This policy explains what Beside does and does not collect.

What we do not collect

Beside does not require an account. We do not sell user data, and we do not use advertising networks inside the app. We do not build a profile of your behavior for marketing or analytics.

What stays on your device

Beside stores documents, notes, photos, imported files, and related local data on your device so the app can function. This includes things like:

  • Saved files and imported images
  • Notes and extracted text
  • Thumbnails and local metadata
  • Your personal organization and search data inside the app

Text extraction

If you use Beside to extract text from a document or image, that processing happens on-device. The extracted text is used for viewing, editing, and searching within the app. It is not used to create a personal profile.

Permissions

Beside may ask for access to device features needed to import and organize content, such as photo access or file access. These permissions are used only to provide the features you choose to use in the app.

External links

If you open a link saved in Beside, that website is governed by its own privacy practices. Beside does not control or monitor activity on third-party websites.

Retention

Data remains in the app until you delete it or remove the app. If you delete an item, it is removed from the app's local storage.

Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, email us at zainworkmaill@gmail.com.

This policy may be updated from time to time. Continued use of Beside after any update indicates your acceptance of the updated policy.
